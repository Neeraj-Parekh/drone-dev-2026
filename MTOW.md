### Maximum Take-Off Weight (MTOW) Definition

Maximum Take-Off Weight (MTOW) is the maximum structural and aerodynamic mass at which an aircraft (or UAV) is certified to attempt flight. Exceeding this limit compromises structural integrity, degrades maneuverability, and risks propulsion system thermal or electrical overload.

---

### 1. Mass Decomposition Formula

For a battery-powered agricultural [[multirotor]], MTOW is mathematically defined as:

$MTOW = m_{dry} + m_{payload} + m_{battery}$

Where:

* $m_{dry}$ (Operating Empty Weight / OEW): Mass of the structural frame, motors, ESCs, flight controller, wiring, pumps, and empty tank.
* $m_{payload}$: Mass of the active liquid chemical payload (density $\approx 1.0 \text{ kg/L}$ for water-based mixtures).
* $m_{battery}$: Mass of the semi-solid-state lithium polymer battery packs.

---

### 2. Key Related Terms & Physics Formulas

#### [[Thrust-to-Weight Ratio]] ($TWR$)

Determines the control authority and acceleration capacity of the aircraft. For industrial agricultural spraying drones, a $TWR \ge 2.0$ is mandatory to counteract wind shears and payload sloshing.

$$TWR = \frac{T_{max}}{MTOW \cdot g}$$

Where:

* $T_{max}$: Total combined maximum thrust generated by all $N$ rotors at $100\%$ throttle (in Newtons, $\text{N}$).
* $g$: Acceleration due to gravity ($9.81 \text{ m/s}^2$).
* $MTOW$: Mass in kilograms ($\text{kg}$).

For a hexacopter ($N = 6$):


$$T_{max} = 6 \cdot T_{rotor\_max}$$

#### Disk Loading ($DL$)

The ratio of aircraft weight to the total swept area of the propellers. Higher disk loading reduces aerodynamic efficiency but improves wind resistance.

$$DL = \frac{MTOW \cdot g}{A_{total}} = \frac{MTOW \cdot g}{6 \cdot \left(\frac{\pi D^2}{4}\right)}$$

Where:

* $A_{total}$: Total combined disc area of all propellers ($\text{m}^2$).
* $D$: Propeller diameter ($\text{m}$).

#### Induced Hover Velocity ($v_i$)

Based on Momentum Theory (Actuator Disk Theory), the downward velocity imparted to the air column during hover is:

$$v_i = \sqrt{\frac{MTOW \cdot g}{2 \rho A_{total}}}$$

Where:

* $\rho$: Air density ($\approx 1.225 \text{ kg/m}^3$ at sea level, standard temperature and pressure).

#### Power Required to Hover ($P_{hover}$)

The electrical power required to maintain a static hover at a given MTOW:

$$P_{hover} = \frac{MTOW \cdot g}{\eta_{sys}}$$

Where:

* $\eta_{sys}$: Overall system efficiency in Newtons per Watt ($\text{N/W}$), calculated as $\eta_{sys} = \eta_{propeller} \cdot \eta_{motor} \cdot \eta_{esc}$.
