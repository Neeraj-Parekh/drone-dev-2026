The anomalous "10L Tank Weight" (EFT-10L Trap)The Issue: In the database, the eft_10l_tank is listed at 5,800g, while the larger eft_16l_tank is listed at 1,800g (a 4.0 kg weight savings for a larger tank).The Cause: The 10L tank's listed weight includes the heavy aluminum battery tray and landing gear mount assembly, while the 16L listing reflects the bare plastic shell.The Correction: For weight-critical builds, we specify the eft_16l_tank (1.8 kg) with a restricted liquid fill, or a custom lightened eft_10l_tank with all non-essential brackets removed.B. The PDB Output Port Deficit (Preset 7 Octocopter)The Issue: Specifying the tarot_tl2996_pdb for an 8-motor octocopter.The Cause: The Tarot PDB has exactly 6 ESC outputs (esc_outputs: 6). Physically routing 8 ESC lines into this board would require unsafe splitting of the high-current busbar.The Correction: Upgraded the power distribution system to the matek_hv_pdb (FCHUB-12S), which natively features 8 independent ESC outputs and an integrated 200A shunt.C. The Motor-to-Arm Mount Mismatch (Preset 8 Mapping)The Issue: Pairing the tarot_t960_frame (22mm carbon fiber arm tubes) with sunnysky_x4112s_motor (46mm motor outer diameter).The Cause: Standard 22mm Tarot clamps cannot accept the mounting bolt circles of a 46mm motor without custom-machined aluminum plates, introducing structural weight and vibration.The Correction: Swapped the frame to the eft_e610p_frame (35mm native arm clamps), allowing direct, secure mounting of the SunnySky motors.2.
