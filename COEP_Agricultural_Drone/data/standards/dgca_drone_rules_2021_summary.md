# DGCA Drone Rules 2021 — Summary

**Source:** dronevex.in, zbotic.in, dgca.gov.in

## Drone Categories

| Category | Weight Range |
|---|---|
| Nano | <250g |
| Micro | 250g–2kg |
| Small | 2–25kg |
| Medium | 25–150kg |
| Large | >150kg |

## Key Provisions

- **Registration:** Digital Sky platform mandatory for Micro and above
- **UIN:** Unique Identification Number displayed on drone
- **NPNT:** No Permission No Takeoff — digital permission system
- **RPL:** Remote Pilot License mandatory for commercial operations
- **Insurance:** Third-party liability mandatory (Rule 44)

## Airspace Classification

| Zone | Description |
|---|---|
| Green | Basic operations permitted |
| Yellow | Restricted — requires special permission |
| Red | Prohibited |

## Operational Limits

- **Max Altitude:** 120m AGL in green zone
- **VLOS:** Visual Line of Sight required

## Frequency Rules

- **865–867 MHz:** ISM band for telemetry (approved)
- **915 MHz:** BANNED in India

## Penalties

- Up to ₹1,00,000 fine
- Drone confiscation
