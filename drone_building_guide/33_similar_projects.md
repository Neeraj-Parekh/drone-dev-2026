## Indian Agricultural Drone Projects

### 1. DJI Agras T30
- **Tank**: 30L
- **Coverage**: 50 acres/hour
- **Features**: AI path planning, RTK positioning, obstacle avoidance
- **Use**: Precision spraying, seeding

### 2. DJI Agras T100
- **Tank**: 100L
- **Coverage**: 100+ acres/hour
- **Features**: Heavy payload, autonomous operation
- **Use**: Large-scale commercial agriculture

### 3. Garuda Aerospace Kisan Drone
- **Tank**: 10L
- **Coverage**: 30 acres/day
- **Certification**: DGCA-certified
- **Location**: Chennai, India
- **Use**: Smallholder farming, government schemes

### 4. Marut AG-365S
- **Tank**: 16L
- **Flight time**: 22 minutes
- **Features**: Multi-utility (spray, seeding, data collection)
- **Distinction**: India's first multi-utility agri drone
- **Location**: Hyderabad, India

### 5. insideFPV Krishi Drone
- **Tank**: 10L
- **Flow rate**: 2–3 L/min
- **Video range**: 10km
- **GCS**: Android-based
- **Location**: Gujarat, India

### 6. XAG P40 / P150
- **Tank**: 10L (P40) / 15L (P150)
- **Features**: Intelligent flight planning, autonomous operation
- **Coverage**: 15–40 acres/hour
- **Origin**: International (China)

### 7. Hylio AG-272
- **Tank**: 27L (dual tank)
- **Type**: Heavy-duty hexacopter
- **Features**: High payload capacity
- **Origin**: USA

### 8. Robocraft 10L Hexacopter
- **Cost**: ₹2,47,800
- **Navigation**: GPS waypoint
- **Build**: Open-source friendly
- **Use**: Custom agricultural applications
